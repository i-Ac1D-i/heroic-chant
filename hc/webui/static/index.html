Inventory

Gear, runes, costumes and chests are set like currencies. Relics and Artifacts are owned one at a time, so search adds one copy instead. Pick a category to browse its whole catalogue.

Roster

Pull rates

Relative weights per rarity, per banner. They do not have to add up to anything — 845/130/25 and 84.5/13/2.5 behave identically. Leave a banner untouched to use the client's own unitGachaID rates. Banner 2 is the one that supplies the hero pools; banner 1 (the Time Cube) has no pools of its own and falls back to it.

Duplicate heroes

What you get when you summon a hero you already own. Rows are [type1, type2, type3, amount]. Put "unit" in the type2 slot to mean the hero that was actually summoned.
Defaults: A and S give a generic Memory Stone from the client's DecompPieceReturn table; SS and SSS give 40 of that hero's own Memory — [16, "unit", -1, 40] — which is exactly what one star-up costs in GradeUpMaterial.

Summon cost

Per banner. Blank uses GachaList.

Stage drops

Multiplies every stage reward. 1.0 is what the game shipped with.

Per-stage overrides

Replace a stage's drops outright. JSON object keyed by dungeon id, each an array of [type1, type2, type3, amount]. Example: {"1": [[4,-1,-1,1000]]}

How the ladder is filled

Arena is asynchronous — you fight a snapshot of somebody's defence team, so they never have to be online. The client shipped with … ready-made AI teams, which means a one-player server already has a full ladder. Anyone else who connects to this server joins it automatically and can be found and fought.

Your bot teams

Teams you build here are offered alongside the shipped ones — or instead of them, if you turn those off above. A team needs a name, a rating (which decides who it gets matched against) and at least one hero.

Device links

Every guest device gets its own account the first time it connects, so several people can share one server without sharing a save. Re-point a device here to move someone onto a different save — that is the other half of import/export.
